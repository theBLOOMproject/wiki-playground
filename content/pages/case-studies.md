::: keypoints
- Three real Central Oregon decisions show how the public’s role plays out in practice.
- Each case looks at the decision, who had authority, the public’s role, competing views, and what happened afterward.
:::

The case studies below show how real AI-related decisions have played out in Central Oregon. For each one, we look at:

- **The decision:** What was being decided?
- **Who had authority:** Who made the final call?
- **The public’s role:** When and where could the public influence it?
- **Competing views:** What did different people think?
- **What happened afterward?**

::: cards
- [Bend-La Pine School District’s AI use policy](#case-bend-la-pine) — How a school district shaped its AI rules with stakeholders, and what changed after a parent petition.
- [Data centers in Prineville vs. La Pine](#case-data-centers) — Two Central Oregon communities, two very different outcomes when data centers came calling.
- [Bend’s license plate cameras](#case-license-plate-cameras) — How public pressure changed course on AI cameras that were already in use. A proposed third case.
:::
